“Paano po mag-take ng CSE? Paano po yung process?”

1. First of all, kailangan mong maka-secure ng slot. Ito po yung part na magfi-fill out ka ng form with your personal details at pipili ka ng gusto mong schedule for your appointment, para sa pagpasa ng requirements ng personal.

2. “Saan po ba kukuha ng slot? Online po ba? Walk-in?” It depends po kung saang region ka mag-ti-take ng exam. Pwedeng sa online appointment/booking system na eServe, OCSEAS, PickTime (usually for Bulacan), sariling online appointment/booking system ng CSC sa region, or walk-in. Please refer to the image below. (image source: CSC official announcement, page.)

3. I highly recommend po na i-follow ninyo ang official Facebook page ng inyong CSC Regional Office para updated po kayo sa mga CSE-related announcements.
