Requirements

• If kumuha or nag-secure ka po ng slot through E-SERVE or OCSEAS, may nag-generate na dun na application form with your info/details na. So, no need na po mag-fill out ng handwritten. Kailangan mo na lang i-download at i-print sa long bond paper, back-to-back. Isang copy lang po ang required, but you may print and bring 2 copies just in case.

• If kailangan mo ng copy ng application form for handwritten fill-out, you may download it here: (source: https://csc.gov.ph/downloads/category/459-cse-application-form)

⁍ APPLICATION FORM (Career Service-Professional)
⁍ APPLICATION FORM (Career Service-SubProfessional)

• Passport size, with name tag.

• Pag magpapapicture ka po sa mga photo studio, sabihin mo lang na gagamitin for Civil Service Exam. Alam na nila ’yon. Para sa complete details ng required ID picture, please refer to the guide/image below. (image source: CSC official announcement, page.)

• Kailangan mong dalhin ang original valid ID mo at photocopy. I suggest na dalawang photocopies na, just in case. Long bond paper, pwedeng colored or black, basta readable. Para sa complete list ng acceptable na valid IDs na puwedeng dalhin at i-present, refer to the guide/images below. (image source: CSC official announcement, page.)

• If walang birth date ang valid id mo, you can bring your birth certificate and a photocopy. (image source: CSC official announcement, page.)

• Of course, ang ₱500.00. After mo magpasa ng mga requirements, may mare-receive ka na 2 receipts. Yung receipt ng binayaran mo na 500 at yung application receipt.
